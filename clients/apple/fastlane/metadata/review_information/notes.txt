Moonrise is a player for the user's own media server (the free, open-source Moonrise server they run at home, e.g. on a NAS). Reviewers don't have one, so the app has a built-in demo: on the first screen tap Try the Demo (Apple TV: move down to Try the Demo at the bottom of the sign-in screen and press Select). It shows four Creative Commons films by the Blender Foundation (Big Buck Bunny, Elephants Dream, Sintel, Tears of Steel), streamed from their public hosts (archive.org, download.blender.org): browse Home and Library, open a title, play it, choose subtitles (Tears of Steel: English and more; Sintel: French, Dutch, Russian) and the audio track in the player. Exit Demo is on Home (iPhone/iPad/Mac) and in Settings → Demo. No account, sign-in or purchase exists in the app. The server software is at https://github.com/paralainer/moonrise (setup: run the Docker image, the app finds it on the local network and walks through a 6-digit setup code). The app uses the local network only to find and talk to the user's server (NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription).

About the app (the questions Apple asks of new developer accounts):
- Purpose: a player for the user's own video library, served by the free, open-source Moonrise server they install on their own computer or NAS at home. The app has no content of its own; the demo's films are Creative Commons works by the Blender Foundation.
- Audience: people who keep their own films and shows and run a home media server, and their family members, whom the server's owner invites.
- Services: the app talks only to the user's own Moonrise server, on the local network or through the user's own Tailscale network. There is no account with the developer, no server run by the developer, no in-app purchase, no ads and no analytics. The demo streams its films from archive.org and download.blender.org.
- Regions: all regions; the app is free.
- The same app runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV (Mac: same demo, Try the Demo on the welcome window).
