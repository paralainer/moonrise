Credits for the sample films

The screenshots, the screen recording and the clip on the Moonrise page show the apps with a library of Blender open movies. The films, their posters and stills are not Moonrise's: they belong to their makers and are used under the licences below. The apps' own interface in the screenshots is Moonrise's (see the repository's licences and TRADEMARKS.md).

The screenshots and the recording were taken from the Moonrise apps on simulators, against a server whose library held these films with the posters and stills listed here as local artwork. No image came from TMDB or any other metadata service. Plot summaries in the library and on the page are our own.

Changes

  • Posters and stills were scaled, cropped and re-encoded. The Caminandes still was cropped to a portrait poster.
  • The clip at the top of the page is an excerpt: 28 seconds of Tears of Steel (from 0:12), scaled to 854 pixels wide, cropped to 2.39:1 on wide screens and re-encoded (MP4 and WebM). Its subtitles (film/tos-en.vtt) are lines from the film's English subtitles, retimed to the clip.
  • The screenshots and the screen recording show the films inside the apps, scaled and cropped with the app's screen.
  • Nothing else was changed.
