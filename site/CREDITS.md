# Credits for the landing page's sample media

The site serves the same credits as a page, [`credits.html`](credits.html), which the page's footer
links to; keep the two in step (`scripts/tests/site.test.sh` checks that they name the same sources).

The screenshots and the screen recording in `shots/` and the clip in `film/` show the Moonrise apps
with a library of Blender open movies. The films, their posters and stills are
not Moonrise's: they belong to their makers and are used here under the licences below. The
apps' own interface in the screenshots is Moonrise's (see the repository's licences and
[TRADEMARKS.md](../TRADEMARKS.md)).

The screenshots and the recording were taken from the Moonrise apps on simulators, against a server whose library
held these films with the posters and stills listed here as local artwork. No image came from
TMDB or any other metadata service. Plot summaries in the library and on the page are our own.

## Changes

Posters and stills were scaled, cropped and re-encoded; the *Caminandes* still was cropped to a
portrait poster. The clip `film/tos-clip.mp4` / `.webm` is an excerpt: 28 seconds of *Tears of
Steel* (from 0:12), scaled to 854 px wide and re-encoded; the page crops it to 2.39:1 on wide
screens. Its subtitles, `film/tos-en.vtt`, are lines from the film's English subtitles, retimed to
the clip. The screenshots and the recording show the films inside the apps, scaled and cropped with
the app's screen. Nothing else was changed.
