# Trademarks

The code licences (MPL-2.0 and AGPL-3.0, see the [README](README.md#license)) cover the code and
docs. They don't cover the name "Moonrise" or the Moonrise icon and logo: the artwork in
`assets/brand/` and the app icons, banners and marks made from it in the apps' asset folders.

## What's fine

- Building and running Moonrise unmodified, and sharing unmodified builds under its own name.
- Plain factual references: "works with Moonrise", "a client for the Moonrise server", "based on
  Moonrise", in text, reviews, articles and docs.

## What needs its own name and icon

- A fork, or any modified build you give to others, including builds published on the App Store,
  Google Play or another store. Change the app name, bundle or application id, and icons before
  distributing it, and don't present it as Moonrise or as coming from the Moonrise project.

If you are unsure whether a use is fine, open an issue and ask.
